How to Play Hex Duel

1. Objective: Take turns placing your colored hexagon on any empty cell. Lime connects the Top & Bottom edges; Rose connects the Left & Right edges.

2. No Draws Possible: The first player to form an unbroken chain touching both of their colored borders wins immediately.

3. Pie (Swap) Rule: Because going first is an advantage, on Move 2 the second player may click Swap First Move to take over the first stone.

4. Controls: Click/tap any hex to place. Use the bottom Zoom slider to zoom in, drag to pan when zoomed, and use ← / → to review move history.
